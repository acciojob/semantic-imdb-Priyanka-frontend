Inception is a science fiction thriller directed by Christopher Nolan. The story follows Dom Cobb, a skilled thief who enters people's dreams to steal secrets. He is offered a chance to erase his criminal history by performing an impossible task called "inception" — planting an idea into someone's mind.

The movie stars Leonardo DiCaprio as Dom Cobb, Joseph Gordon-Levitt as Arthur, and Ellen Page as Ariadne. Their performances made the movie unforgettable.
